I am a PhD student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Jiayi Huang. Previously, I received my MPhil in Microelectronics from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) in 2025, where I was advised by Prof. Jiayi Huang and Prof. Yuzhe Ma, and my BEng in Microelectronics Science and Engineering from Sichuan University in 2023.

My research interests lie in the fields of computer architecture and systems, with a special focus on scale-up interconnection networks for large-scale AI systems.
